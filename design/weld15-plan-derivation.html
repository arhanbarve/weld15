How the plan was derived, and which numbers to trust

Every dimension carries a tag: it came from the resident's email, it follows by reasoning from something that did, or I inferred it and you should treat it as a dial. In this revision the layout closes to the foot on all three axes, and space I cannot account for is left blank rather than assigned to a room.

Reading the drawings
Two axes, one band, and which walls are real

Weld Hall is long and narrow: about 54 feet across, 151 feet end to end, long axis tilted 13 degrees east of north. Your suite is a band across the narrow direction at the north end. Only two measurements matter — how far the band runs across the building, and how deep it reaches back from the end wall.

The band, unlabellednorth gable at top
Depth is 20 ft, and the resident told us without meaning to. He gave the common room as "15 ft by 15-20 ft" — one number confident, one a range. A range appears when you are reading a plan and cannot tell where a wall lands. The common room runs from the end wall back to the corridor, so its uncertain dimension is the band depth.

The plan
Weld 15
Scale 1/8 in = 1 ft · walls to true thickness
Walls are generated, not drawn. Every wall on this plan comes out of one function that takes the room widths and emits masonry and partitions as solid bands with openings punched through them. No room outlines, so no doubled-up strokes where two rooms meet. The same function becomes walls.ts in the build, which is why this drawing is worth getting exactly right. Blank means unknown. Two regions are hatched and carry no room name: about 7 x 20 ft at the west end, and 7 x 7 ft behind the bathroom. The rooms account for 44 of Weld's 51 ft clear width, and I am not naming the rest. Earlier drafts called the west strip an elevator and gave the 7 x 7 to bedroom A. Both were guesses wearing facts' clothes.

A separate question
Where that band sits inside Weld

Room arrangement and position in the building are two decisions, and I previously ran them together. Arrangement is settled. Position is not, and cannot be from public sources — Harvard's indoor plans are behind a login.
