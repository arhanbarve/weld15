# P10 — four sessions, one phase

P10 was built as four concurrent worktrees cut from the same commit (`8e6ef50`), each answering a
different part of one brief, each with its own spec and its own implementation plan. They were
merged onto `p10-merge` afterwards. This file is the index; it is not a summary, because a summary
of four specs is a fifth document that nobody would keep true.

| document | branch | what it answers |
|---|---|---|
| [`P10-UX.md`](./P10-UX.md) | `p10-ux` | The controls. One dock top right at every stage, one master scrubber for the whole descent, a globe you can turn, wheel-driven zoom, the stage-0 marker shrunk to a pin, stage 3's arrow buttons retired, the sun folded away, high contrast retired, the Sources panel dismissible. |
| [`P10-INTERIOR.md`](./P10-INTERIOR.md) | `p10-fidelity` | The room. Furniture built from parts rather than boxed, sashes in the interior windows, baseboard/picture rail/cornice, brick and plaster with an environment to reflect — and stage 4 reworked so it can be dragged the way stage 3 can. |
| [`P10-EXTERIOR.md`](./P10-EXTERIOR.md) | `p10-imagery` | The ground and the neighbours. Leaf-on colour imagery through the whole pyramid, an L4 hybrid that keeps MassGIS's 15 cm detail and NAIP's colour, and Harvard's own building meshes in place of the translucent blue boxes. |
| [`P10-WALK-IN.md`](./P10-WALK-IN.md) | `p10-walk-in` | The room, on foot. No click-to-teleport, no stand-up toggle — you arrive standing — and a head that pitches, so you can look at the floor. |

Two of the four wrote a separate build order beside the spec, and both are kept under the names
their own files and scripts already refer to: `P10-IMPLEMENTATION.md` is `p10-fidelity`'s, and
`P10-IMPL.md` is `p10-ux`'s — the one `scripts/p10-measure.mjs` cites by name for its selector
list, its crimson test and its sampling stride. `P10-EXTERIOR-PLAN.md` is `p10-imagery`'s and
`P10-WALK-IN-PLAN.md` is `p10-walk-in`'s.

Two of these overlapped on purpose and were reconciled at the merge rather than in flight. Both
records are in the documents themselves:

- **`P10-UX.md` §S6 and `P10-EXTERIOR.md` Part C both make the campus solid and the ground
  colour.** `P10-UX.md` §8 says S6 is deliberately the last step so it can be dropped, and it was:
  the exterior branch's real Harvard geometry (`CampusMesh.tsx`) supersedes the aerial-skinned box
  massing, and its imagery pipeline supersedes the tint-constant change. What survives from S6 is
  its cleanup — the dead opacity ramp and the gates that asserted it.
- **`P10-INTERIOR.md` §D4 put the HUD at the top for stage 4.** `P10-UX.md` §S1 puts the dock top
  right at every stage, which answers the same measurement without a per-stage rule.
